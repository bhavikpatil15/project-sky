The design should feel like a professional planetary defense operations center, inspired by NASA mission control, ESA dashboards, and modern aerospace interfaces. Avoid a gaming aesthetic. Use a clean, minimalist layout with subtle neon blue and cyan accents, soft glows, glassmorphism panels, and a starfield background with faint orbital grid lines.
